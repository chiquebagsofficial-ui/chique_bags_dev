1. Introduction

Further to our discussions, the Developer is pleased to submit this quotation for the design, development and launch of the official online store of Chique Bags. The store will give the brand a home of its own beyond Instagram, allowing customers to browse the collection, place orders and pay online, while the Proprietor manages the catalogue, orders and customers independently through a private administration panel.

Section 2 defines the scope of work. Sections 3 to 8 set out the technology, commercial terms, support arrangements, responsibilities, exclusions and general conditions that apply to it.

2. Scope of work

2.2 Administration panel

A private, separately authenticated panel through which the Proprietor operates the store.

2.3 Orders, payments and stock

  1. The server recalculates every price, discount, shipping and gift charge from the database; figures sent by the browser are never relied upon.
  2. For online payment, the server creates the order with Razorpay and verifies Razorpay's signature on completion. A webhook also confirms the order if the customer's connection is lost after payment, and duplicate notifications are processed once only.
  3. Stock is reduced when an order is confirmed and can never fall below zero. Where two customers pay for the last piece simultaneously, the second order is flagged to the Proprietor for resolution.
  4. Cancelling an order restores its stock and refunds an online payment through Razorpay.

2.4 Automated emails

The following emails are sent without manual action: verification codes (sign-up, guest checkout, password reset); welcome; order confirmation itemising prices, discounts, shipping, gift box and total; order shipped, with a tracking link; order delivered; order cancelled, with refund information; new-order alerts and contact-form messages to the Proprietor; and an acknowledgement to the customer for each contact message.

Rate limiting on login, sign-up, codes, checkout, contact and tracking. All input validated on the server, database queries parameterised and user-supplied text escaped. Secure browser headers, including HTTPS enforcement. Keys and passwords held in protected server settings.

3. Technology and third-party services

The website is a custom build rather than a template, using the following technologies and services.

ComponentTechnology
WebsiteNext.js, React and TypeScript, styled with Tailwind CSS
DatabasePostgreSQL on Neon, accessed through Prisma
PaymentsRazorpay (prepaid orders)
PhotographsCloudinary
EmailSMTP email service
HostingVercel, with a custom domain

These services are to be held in accounts owned by the Client. Their subscription fees, the domain registration fee and Razorpay's transaction charges are payable by the Client directly and do not form part of the quoted price.

3.1 Database backups and recovery

The database is hosted on Neon, a managed PostgreSQL service that keeps a continuous history of changes and can restore the database to an earlier point in time. The length of that history depends on the Neon plan chosen by the Client. The Developer will document the recovery procedure and can export an independent copy of the database on request. Photographs are stored on Cloudinary and the source code on GitHub, from which the website can be redeployed at any time.

4. Delivery of content

The website will be delivered with sample products and stock photographs so that the design may be reviewed. The Client's own product, banner and gift box photographs, product details and prices are to replace them before launch. Products, prices, stock and photographs may thereafter be updated at any time through the administration panel.

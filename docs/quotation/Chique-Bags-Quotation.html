Project overview

This quotation covers the design and development of the official online store for Chique Bags, a handcrafted bag brand owned by Priyanka Vora, for a total of ₹10,000.

The store gives Chique Bags a home of its own outside Instagram. Customers can discover the collection, save favourites, pay securely online or by cash on delivery, and track their orders. The owner manages products, orders, customers and offers from a private admin panel, with no developer needed for day-to-day work.

The sections below list everything included in the price.

Customer storefront

Everything a shopper sees and uses, built mobile-first so it works as well on a phone as on a laptop. The look follows the brand's soft pink, cream and gold palette, with a refined, editorial design: large photography, fine serif headings, spaced capitals and generous white space, like a boutique fashion label's own website.

Homepage

  • Announcement bar and a sticky menu with a Shop dropdown, search, favourites, account and bag, plus a bottom tab bar on mobile.
  • Full-width hero banner slider with two to three different banners (for example the new collection, gifting and bestsellers). Each banner has its own photo, headline, text and buttons, slides automatically, and can be swiped on a phone. The owner adds, edits, reorders and hides banners from the admin panel, with a separate phone photo if needed.
  • Shop by style tiles, Bestsellers, New Arrivals and an "Under ₹999" spotlight, all updating automatically as products change in the admin panel.
  • Gifting section with a photo of the Chique Bags gift box, explaining that a gift box and a handwritten note can be added to any purchase.
  • Brand story section, Instagram-style lookbook linking to @chiquebagsofficial, trust strip (vegan leather, free shipping, easy exchange, payment options).
  • Footer with contact details, policy links and an email sign-up form.

Browsing and finding products

  • Shop page with category filters, price filters, an in-stock-only filter, sorting (newest, best sellers, price low to high and high to low) and pagination. Filtered pages have shareable links.
  • Instant search with product suggestions, and a full search results page.
  • Product cards show sale, new, bestseller and sold-out tags, colour swatches, MRP, sale price and the percentage off, with a second photo on hover.

Product page

  • Photo gallery with thumbnails, and breadcrumbs back to the category.
  • Colour selector with stock tracked per colour, sold-out colours clearly marked, and a "Only 3 left" low-stock notice.
  • Sale price with "you save" amount, quantity selector, Add to bag, Buy it now and a favourites button.
  • Description, details, care and shipping sections, plus related products.
  • Customer reviews and star ratings. Only customers whose order was delivered can review.

Gifting: gift box and note

  • A dedicated Gifting page with the gift box photo, a three-step explanation, most-gifted bags and common questions.
  • At checkout, a customer can add the signature gift box to any purchase with one tick, and write a gift note of up to 200 characters. The gift box price is added to the total and shown on the bill, invoice and emails.
  • The owner sets the gift box price (or makes it free), switches the option on or off, and changes the gift box photo from the admin panel.
  • Gift orders are highlighted in the admin panel with the note to write on the card, and the gift box and note appear in the order emails, invoice and the customer's order page.

Favourites

  • Heart button on every product. Favourites are kept on the device for visitors and saved to the account for logged-in customers, then merged when someone logs in.

Bag and checkout

  • Slide-out bag with a free-shipping progress bar, plus a full bag page. The bag is remembered on the customer's device.
  • Quantity controls, original price, sale price and savings shown for each item.
  • Checkout with a live summary: total at MRP, discount, coupon, shipping and final total.
  • Guest checkout verified by a one-time email code, or checkout from a logged-in account.
  • Saved delivery addresses and coupon codes, and the optional gift box and note.
  • Online payment through Razorpay (UPI, cards, net banking and wallets), and cash on delivery that the owner can switch on or off.
  • If a price changes while the customer is checking out, they are shown the new total before paying.
  • Order confirmation and payment-failed pages, and a downloadable invoice.

Customer account

  • Sign up with email verification, login, and forgot or reset password by email code.
  • Profile, change password and saved addresses.
  • Order history with full details, a progress tracker, tracking link, invoice download and the option to cancel an order that has not shipped.
  • Track Order page where anyone can check an order with the order number and email.

Information pages

  • About us, Contact (form that reaches the owner by email and in the admin panel), Shipping and Delivery, Returns and Exchange, Terms and Conditions, and Privacy Policy, with the business details needed for payment-gateway approval.

Admin panel

A private panel for the owner to run the store day to day, protected by its own login.

Dashboard

  • Total revenue, orders, customers and live products, a seven-day sales chart, today's sales, the latest orders and a list of colours that are running low on stock.
  • An alert when something needs attention, for example a customer paid for the last piece at the same moment another customer did.

Products

  • Add, edit, hide and delete products. Name, category, descriptions, highlights, material, dimensions and care instructions.
  • Original price and sale price, with the discount worked out automatically.
  • Colours with their own stock, and photo upload straight from the owner's device to cloud storage with a choice of cover photo.
  • Flags for New, Bestseller and Featured on the homepage.

Orders

  • Search by order number, name, email or phone, and filter by status. Open any order for its items, customer, address and payment.
  • Move an order from confirmed to packing, shipped and delivered, and add the courier name and tracking link. The customer is emailed automatically when an order ships or is delivered.
  • Cancelling an order puts its stock back on sale, releases the coupon and refunds an online payment automatically.
  • Printable invoice for every order, including the gift box line. On the order screen, gift orders show a clear "pack in a gift box" reminder with the note text to write.

Customers

  • List of customers with email, phone, number of orders, total spent and joining date, with search.

Also included in the panel

  • Categories: create and reorder the categories shown on the shop.
  • Coupons: percentage or flat-amount codes with a minimum order, usage limits, expiry date, one use per customer and an on/off switch.
  • Messages: an inbox of contact-form messages.
  • Banners: add, edit, reorder and switch off the homepage hero banners, with photo upload.
  • Settings: announcement bar text, free-shipping amount and shipping fee, cash on delivery on/off, gift box on/off, price and photo, contact details and the email address for order alerts.

Payments, orders and emails

Every order moves through a fixed, safe flow.

  1. The customer reviews the bag. The server recalculates every price, discount, coupon and shipping charge from the database.
  2. For online payment, the server creates the payment order with Razorpay and the customer pays in the Razorpay window.
  3. The payment is verified on the server using Razorpay's signature before the order is confirmed.
  4. A second route, the Razorpay webhook, confirms the order if the customer's connection drops after paying. Duplicate notifications are handled safely.
  5. Stock is reduced when the order is confirmed, so the same piece cannot be sold twice.
  6. The customer receives a confirmation email, and the owner receives a new-order alert.

Emails sent automatically

  • One-time verification codes for sign-up, guest checkout and password reset.
  • Welcome email after sign-up.
  • Order confirmation with every item, price, discount, shipping, gift box and note, and total.
  • Shipping notification with a Track Your Order button, and a delivered notification.
  • Cancellation email with refund information.
  • New-order alerts and contact-form messages to the owner, and an acknowledgement to the customer.

Stock rules

  • Stock is tracked per colour, with sold-out colours disabled on the product page.
  • If two customers pay for the last piece at the same moment, stock never goes negative and the owner is alerted.
  • Cancelling an order restores its stock.

Security

The store handles customer details and payments, so security is built in from the start.

  • Separate logins: customer and admin sessions are kept apart. Admin credentials do not work on the storefront, and customers cannot reach the admin panel.
  • Protected admin: every admin page and admin data route is blocked on the server for anyone who is not signed in as the owner.
  • Safe sessions: encrypted, HTTP-only cookies with same-site protection.
  • Passwords are stored only as strong one-way hashes (bcrypt), never as plain text.
  • Email verification codes expire after 10 minutes and lock after repeated wrong attempts.
  • Rate limiting on login, sign-up, code requests, checkout, contact and tracking to stop guessing and spam.
  • No trust in the browser: prices, discounts, coupons, shipping and stock are always recalculated on the server.
  • Input checking: every request is validated on the server, and database access is parameterised to prevent SQL injection. Customer-supplied text is escaped in pages and emails.
  • Payment security: Razorpay signatures are verified on every payment, webhooks are signature-checked and processed only once, and the paid amount is checked against the order.
  • Privacy of orders: customers can see only their own orders and invoices.
  • Browser protections: secure headers on every page, including forced HTTPS, clickjacking protection and a strict referrer policy.
  • Secrets and uploads: keys and passwords live in protected server settings, never in the code. Admin photo uploads are signed by the server.

SEO, speed and accessibility

  • Every page has its own title and description. Shared product links show the photo and name on WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook.
  • Structured product data (name, price, availability) so Google can show rich results, clean readable web addresses, sitemap.xml and robots.txt.
  • Pages are rendered on the server so search engines see the full content. Product photos are resized and served in modern formats automatically.
  • Mobile-first layouts, keyboard-friendly navigation, labelled buttons and form fields, and animations that respect the visitor's reduced-motion setting.

The store is a custom build on modern, widely supported tools, not a template, so it can grow with the brand.

AreaTechnology
WebsiteNext.js, React and TypeScript, styled with Tailwind CSS
DatabasePostgreSQL (Neon), accessed through Prisma
PaymentsRazorpay (online) and cash on delivery
PhotosCloudinary
EmailSMTP email sending (verification codes, order and shipping emails)
HostingVercel, with a custom domain

These services run on accounts owned by Chique Bags (domain, hosting, database, Cloudinary, Razorpay and email). Their own plan fees and Razorpay's transaction charges are paid directly by the client and are not part of this quotation.
